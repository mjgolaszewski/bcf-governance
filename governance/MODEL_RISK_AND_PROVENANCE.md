# Model Risk And Provenance

## Applicability

Use this playbook for forecasts, scoring, financial or medical outputs, compliance outputs, AI outputs, or high-impact decision support.

## Rules

Identify active rule or model versions, inputs, assumptions, source freshness, and reproducibility constraints. User-facing language must not overstate certainty.

## Evidence

Recommended artifacts include versioned rule or policy packs, source provenance fields, effective-date selection, reproducibility notes, and known constraints in release readiness.

Repo evidence: regulated profile references in `template-repo/governance-profile.yml`; phase closeout evidence in `phases/*.yml`.
